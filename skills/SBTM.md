# SBTM — Session-Based Test Management (Bach)

Use during ISTQB **Analysis / experience map** (no Pass/Fail yet).

## Charter
One sentence: what you will explore (module, risk, story AC).

## Time-box
45–90 minutes typical. Stop when charter done or time ends.

## TBS notes
- **T**est: what you tried
- **B**ug: candidate issues (do not final-label REAL_BUG here)
- **S**etup / blocked: environment walls

## Debrief
Update `map/` with click X → screen Y, dropdown contents, alternate paths.
Hand off to Test Designer for NEW cases.
