# AUP — Acceptable use

LIQA is a headed human-on-this-PC agent.

Allowed: your UAT, your shop checkout, your own social accounts, your files — on a machine you own, with credentials you supply, while a human can watch.

Forbidden:

- Captcha-solving farms / selling CAPTCHA bypass
- Mass account creation, credential stuffing, scraping other people’s inboxes
- Inventing OTPs or claiming a hard CAPTCHA was solved when a human gate was used
- Headless silent runs sold as “manual QA”
- Sending bank UAT pixels to a public LLM without the customer’s opt-in

Hard CAPTCHA, SMS, Google 2FA, UAC: pause and wait for the human.
